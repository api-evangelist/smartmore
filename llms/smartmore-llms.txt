# SmartMore

> SmartMore Corporation (思谋科技) is a Hong Kong- and Shenzhen-headquartered industrial AI and machine
> vision company founded in 2019. It builds the SMore ViMo industrial vision platform (ViMo Cloud,
> ViMo Deeplearning, ViMo Studio), industrial large models (IndustryGPT, SMore LrMo), digital
> platforms (Light3D, Data, Gauge, AIoT), and first-party machine vision hardware — smart cameras,
> barcode readers, vision sensors and lenses — under the SmartMoreInside sub-brand.

## API surface

As of 2026-08-28 SmartMore publishes **no public developer program**: no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no public SDK in
any package registry (npm, PyPI, crates.io all miss), and no /.well-known/ discovery documents.
Marketing copy for ViMo Deeplearning states that trained models can be exported "into SDK" with
support for Python, C and C++, but that SDK is delivered with the licensed product and no public
documentation, download or specification for it could be found. Integration is arranged through the
company's contact form.
